# Data attribution

`word.json` and `word.txt` combine two sources:

- The selected vocabulary word and its learning progress come from the user's
  personal DuoCards deck.
- The English definition and example come from English Wiktionary. The JSON
  identifies the source page, attribution, and license for every generated
  entry.

English Wiktionary text is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
and the GFDL. Attribution: English Wiktionary contributors. The linked source
page contains the contributor history for the selected entry.

The raw DuoCards snapshots in `data/cards_snapshot.json` and
`data/sources_snapshot.json` are private diagnostics and are excluded from Git.
They must not be published.
